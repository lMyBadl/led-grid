# Understanding BMI 323:
## Vocab:
Bandwidth: Highest frequency that can be sampled without aliasing.
Aliasing: Where high frequency signals measured at a slower frequency look like lower frequency signals
LSB/_: Least significant bits per unit of _, the higher this number the greater your measurement accuracy

## Data:
### Reading Data:
BMI323 sends 1 dummy byte for SPI and 2 dummy bytes for I2C before being able to read data. See pages 14-15 for more info on reading SPI.

Sent with two's complement, 16 bits

## Device Operation:
Enters Suspend power operation mode after Power on Reset. See page 21 for power mode configurations

Default Accel Range: 8g
